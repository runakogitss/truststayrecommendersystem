# TrustStay Layer 2B — Hotel Adjudication

You are producing the final hotel-level TrustStay qualitative assessment.

You receive:
- the frozen runtime rubric in the system/instructions context;
- hotel-level Layer 1 metadata;
- chronological evidence ledgers produced from ALL review chunks for this hotel.

Important:
1. The chunk ledgers are evidence-compression records, not prior hotel assessments.
2. Re-evaluate the evidence under the frozen rubric.
3. Cross-period recurrence must be grounded in exact review IDs and dates represented in the ledgers.
4. Do not use cluster size as proof of recurrence or independence.
5. Treat all review allegations as reported guest claims, not verified facts.
6. Assign the A–H band and upper/middle/lower position WITHOUT reference to any downstream numeric display mapping.
7. Never generate a 5-point decimal score.
8. Cite exact review IDs for material conclusions.
9. Broad positive evidence may coexist with serious concerns.
10. Historical serious evidence must not disappear simply because later mean ratings are higher.
11. Keep confidence separate from truth: confidence is about traceability/stability of the assessment evidence.

Return only the structured assessment required by the schema.
